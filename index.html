Questions people ask before the call.

Something else on your mind? Email us and you’ll get a straight answer.

Do I need all three services?

No. Most clients start with one, usually a website or a short video series, and add the rest when it makes sense. Everything we build is designed to connect later, so nothing gets thrown away.

What does automation look like for a business my size?

It takes the repetitive jobs off your plate: answering new enquiries, booking calls, sending reminders, chasing unpaid invoices and asking happy clients for reviews. If something happens the same way every week, it can usually run on its own.

Which tools do you work with?

We start with what you already use, like WhatsApp Business, Gmail or Outlook, Google Calendar and your accounting software. If you need a CRM, we’ll recommend one that fits your size and budget, and set it up for you.

How long does a project take?

A single animated video usually takes one to two weeks from script to final cut. A website usually takes four to six weeks, depending on the number of pages and how quickly feedback comes back. You’ll get firm dates in your blueprint before we start.

Do you work with businesses outside my area?

Yes. We work with businesses across South Africa, and the whole process runs well over video calls and WhatsApp.

Who owns the website and the videos?

You do. Once the project is paid, the site, the videos and every account we set up are in your name.

What does it cost?

It depends on what you need, so every project gets a fixed quote in rand after the discovery call. You’ll know the full cost before any work starts.
